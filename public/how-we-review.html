Our Process

How every guide is built

Every product we recommend goes through the same rigorous 6-step process. Here's exactly what happens.

01

Discover on Amazon India

We start with the most relevant, actively sold listings in a given budget band. Products must be genuinely available — no out-of-stock picks.

02

Read lakhs of reviews

We filter verified Amazon reviews, YouTube deep-dives, and community forums to understand how each product actually performs after months of use.

03

Compare specs honestly

Processor, RAM, storage, display, thermals — we compare like-for-like and identify where brands cut corners to hit a price point.

04

Cross-check with testing

We reference independent lab tests, hands-on video reviews, and benchmark data to validate real-world performance claims.

05

Rank by real value

We rank by what matters to Indian shoppers — value for money, reliability, after-sales support, and long-term usability.

06

Keep only the best 5

Most products don't make the cut. We keep only the top five per guide — no filler, no padding, no sponsored placements.

Our Ethics

What we stand for

Trust is earned, not claimed. Here's how we protect yours.

Zero Sponsorships

No brand pays us to feature their product. We do not accept free products, gifts, or paid placements in exchange for a recommendation. Our rankings are purely editorial.

Full Transparency

Some links are Amazon affiliate links — meaning we earn a small commission if you buy through them. This never affects our ranking or which products we recommend.

Verified Data Only

Every price, spec, and rating we show is verified from the actual Amazon India listing. We don't inflate specs or hide real trade-offs. Prices are checked at the time of writing.

For Indian Shoppers

We focus on products genuinely available in India, with after-sales support here. No global-only picks, no imports with no warranty. Every recommendation is Indian-market ready.

Our Promise

What you can always expect

Honest specifications: Every processor, RAM, storage, and display spec is checked against the official Amazon listing. No inflated or misleading claims.

Real prices: We show the current Amazon price and MRP, plus EMI options where available. Prices change — always check the live listing before buying.

No paid rankings: A brand can't buy their way to the top of a ShopMaze guide. Rankings are editorial decisions based on value.

Regular updates: Every guide is revisited periodically. Outdated picks get removed. New, better products get added.

Open to correction: If you spot an error, write to us. We correct mistakes transparently and update the guide.
