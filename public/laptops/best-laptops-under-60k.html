Buying Guide

How to Choose a Laptop Under ₹60,000 in 2026

A practical, no-nonsense guide for Indian students, coders, and WFH professionals.

1. Intel Core 3 100U vs AMD Ryzen 3 30 — Which One?

Both processors are entry-level but capable for daily tasks. The Intel Core 3 100U (14th Gen) is newer, more power-efficient, and handles Windows 11's AI features better. The AMD Ryzen 3 30 in the Vivobook Go 15 offers solid multi-tasking and better integrated graphics. For coding and Office work, either works fine — pick based on bundled software and price.

2. RAM: DDR5 vs DDR4 (Matters More Than You Think)

DDR5 RAM (in ASUS Vivobook 14) is around 20–30% faster than DDR4 for multitasking and heavier apps. If you plan to keep the laptop for 3+ years, DDR5 is more future-proof. DDR4 is still fine for basic use — you won't notice the difference in Chrome or Office.

3. Storage: 256GB vs 512GB SSD

At ₹60k, 512GB SSD is the sweet spot — it fits Windows, Office, 50+ apps, and still leaves space for files. 256GB is fine if you mostly work in the cloud or use Google Drive/OneDrive. Almost all these laptops are easy to upgrade later if a slot is available.

4. Display: 60Hz vs 120Hz FHD

The Dell 15 packs a 120Hz FHD IPS panel, which looks noticeably smoother when scrolling, working in sheets, or watching videos. ASUS Vivobook models use standard 60Hz FHD panels — still crisp, just less buttery.

5. Bundled Software = Real Savings

All 5 laptops ship with Windows 11 + Office 2024 or Office H&S 2024, and most include a 1-year Microsoft 365 Basic. Dell 15 Platinum adds 12 months of McAfee. This is ₹6,000–₹10,000 of value you won't pay separately — a big factor in this price bracket.

6. Weight & Portability

The ASUS Vivobook 14 (1.4 kg) is the lightest — best for students who carry laptops every day. The 15.6" models (1.63–1.7 kg) are better for desk work and longer typing sessions. Pick 14" for portability, 15.6" for productivity.

7. Warranty & After-Sales

All 5 picks come with standard 1-year onsite warranty from ASUS or Dell. Dell's service network is slightly wider across small Indian towns; ASUS has strong support in metros. Register your product on the brand's site within 7 days of purchase to activate warranty benefits.
