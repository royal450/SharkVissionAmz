📘 Quick Buying Guide — Kya Check Karein?

RAM

8GB minimum hai is budget mein. Heavy Chrome tabs + Zoom + Word ek saath khologe to thoda lag feel hoga. 16GB ideal hota lekin is range mein rare hai.

Storage

512GB SSD must hai. HDD wale options bilkul avoid karo — speed mein farak clear dikhega.

Processor

Ryzen 3 ya Core 3/i3 level sufficient hai classes + Office ke liye. Heavy coding / Android Studio ke liye thoda weak padega.

Weight & Office

1.6 kg ke around wale better for college bag. Pre-installed Office wale models students ke liye convenient hain — alag se kharidne ki zarurat nahi.

FAQ

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Kya 8GB RAM 2026 mein students ke liye kaafi hai?
Basic use (classes, notes, browsing, light multitasking) ke liye haan. Agar aap bahut saare tabs + software ek saath chalana chahte ho to 16GB better hota, lekin is budget mein 8GB hi milta hai. Most students ke liye manage ho jaata hai.
Inme se sabse best kaunsa hai?
Overall Acer Aspire Lite AL15-41 best lagta hai — price, metal body, Office bundle aur weight ka balance sabse accha hai. Agar aapka budget thoda stretch ho sakta hai to Dell 15 (120Hz + Office) bhi strong option hai.
Kya yeh laptops coding ke liye theek hain?
Light coding (Python, HTML, basic Java, C++) ke liye theek hain. Heavy IDEs + Android Studio + multiple tools ek saath ke liye thoda struggle karega. Engineering students ko ideally 16GB RAM wala dekhna chahiye.
Battery kitne ghante chalegi?
Real use mein 5–7 hours expect karo (online classes + browsing). Full day ke liye power bank ya charging point college mein rakho. Battery life heavily depend karti hai screen brightness aur background apps par.
Dell aur Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3 ₹50,000 se upar hain. Kya include karna chahiye?
Haan, dono around ₹52k hain. Agar aapka budget thoda stretch ho sakta hai to yeh better options hain — especially Dell ka 120Hz display aur Office bundle. Strict ₹50k limit hai to Acer Aspire Lite aur Lenovo V15 G4 pe focus karo.
