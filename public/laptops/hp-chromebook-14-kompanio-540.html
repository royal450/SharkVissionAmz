HP Chromebook 14 Kompanio 540 — FAQ

Can I run Windows software on this Chromebook?

No. It runs ChromeOS, so it uses web apps and Android apps from the Play Store. Full Windows desktop software like the desktop version of MS Office or Adobe apps does not run natively. If Windows apps are essential, this is not the right laptop.

Is 4GB RAM enough for daily use?

For browsing, email, documents and video calls — yes, ChromeOS is efficient. But if you keep many tabs and apps open at once, 4GB will start to feel tight. Light users will be fine; heavy multitaskers should look at 8GB models.

What does the 100GB Google One (1 year) offer include?

It gives you 100GB of Google cloud storage for one year — useful for Drive, Photos and Gmail backups. After the first year, it's a paid subscription unless you cancel, so plan accordingly.

Is the touch display good for daily use?

Yes — the 14 inch HD touch anti-glare display is comfortable for browsing, notes and video. It's HD rather than Full HD, so text is less sharp than on higher-resolution screens, but it's perfectly usable for everyday tasks.
