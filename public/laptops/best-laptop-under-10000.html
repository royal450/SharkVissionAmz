📘 Quick Buying Guide (Kya Check Karein?)

Processor

MediaTek ya Intel Celeron is range mein standard hain. Daily tasks ke liye kaafi hain.

RAM

4GB is price range mein standard hai. Browsing aur basic MS Office ke liye sufficient hai.

OS Choice

Chrome OS: Web apps ke liye best. Windows 11: MS Office ke liye. JioOS: Android + 4G SIM ke liye.

Best For

Online classes, MS Office, browsing, YouTube, email, aur notes banana.
