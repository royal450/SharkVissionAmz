Buying Guide

How to Choose a Laptop Under ₹20,000

A practical, no-nonsense guide for Indian students and first-time buyers.

1. Decide: Windows, Chrome OS, or Android?

At this price range, you get three kinds of laptops:

  • Windows 11 laptops (like Neopticon EBook) — best if you need MS Office offline, desktop apps, or local file storage. Performance is slow but usable for basic tasks.
  • Chrome OS laptops (Acer, HP, Lenovo Chromebooks) — fastest, safest, longest battery life. Best for Google Docs, YouTube, Zoom, browsing, and web apps. No offline MS Office.
  • Android laptops (JioBook 11) — cheapest 4G option. Great for streaming, browsing, and basic apps. Limited for productivity work.

2. Processor: What Actually Matters

At under ₹20,000, you'll see these processors:

  • Intel Celeron N4500 / N4020 — most common, fine for basic tasks.
  • MediaTek Kompanio 540 — good for Chromebooks, efficient on battery.
  • MediaTek 8788 (JioBook) — Android-optimized, best for streaming.

Do not expect gaming, video editing, or heavy Photoshop on any of these.

3. RAM & Storage: 4GB / 64GB Minimum

At this budget, 4GB RAM is the standard — don't buy anything less. For storage, 64GB eMMC is minimum; 128GB UFS (HP Chromebook 14) is noticeably faster. Windows laptops with an M.2 SSD slot (like Neopticon EBook) let you upgrade later — a big plus.

4. Display Size: 11.6" vs 14"

11.6" is lighter and more portable; 14" is better for typing and split-screen work. For students, 11.6" is usually enough. For office work, 14" is more comfortable.

5. Battery Life & Portability

Budget laptops run 6–10 hours on a charge. Chromebooks typically last longest. If you travel a lot, aim for under 1.3 kg — all 5 picks here qualify.
