Is RTX 3050 still good for gaming in 2026?
Yes — for 1080p gaming, the RTX 3050 4GB GDDR6 handles popular titles like Valorant, CS2, GTA V, and Fortnite at medium-to-high settings with 60+ FPS. For AAA titles like Cyberpunk 2077 or Red Dead Redemption 2, you may need to reduce settings. At ₹80,000, RTX 3050 is still the most practical dedicated GPU choice.

For pure gaming, neither has a dedicated GPU — go with Acer ALG RTX 3050 instead.
