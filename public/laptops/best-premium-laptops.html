What is the most powerful laptop in India in 2026?

MacBook Pro M5 Pro vs HP OMEN RTX 5060 — which is more powerful?
For raw CPU + AI workloads — MacBook Pro M5 Pro wins (18-core CPU, 48GB unified memory, better perf/watt). For gaming and CUDA workloads — HP OMEN 16 RTX 5060 wins (dedicated GPU, 165Hz display). Choose MacBook for video editing, coding, ML training; choose OMEN for gaming, 3D rendering with CUDA.
Should I buy the ₹4L MacBook Pro 16 M5 Pro or the ₹92K Dell Ultra 7?
Only 5% of users actually need the MacBook Pro 16" M5 Pro's full power — that's for 8K video, 3D rendering, ML training, professional audio. For 95% of buyers, the Dell 15 AI Ultra 7 255H at ₹92,990 is more than enough — it handles Office, coding, video calls, and light editing effortlessly. Buy the M5 Pro only if you're a professional creator with demanding workloads.
