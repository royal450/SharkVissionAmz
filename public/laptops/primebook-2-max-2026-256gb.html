Final Verdict

Should you buy it?

Buy it if…

You're a student or need a second laptop for light work — browsing, YouTube, online classes, assignments, and Android apps. The 15.6" screen, 12-hour battery, and ₹28,990 price make it one of the best value Android laptops in India [citation:5].

Skip it if…

You need full offline MS Office, do heavy video editing, or play PC games. Also skip if your college/office uses certificate-based Wi-Fi — the Primebook may not connect [citation:3]. For those needs, get a Windows laptop instead.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Primebook 2 Max support MS Office?
Only the online version. You can open Word/Excel/PowerPoint files through a browser, but you can't double-click and edit them offline like on Windows. If MS Office is critical, this laptop is not for you [citation:1][citation:7].
Can I expand the storage?
Yes. The 256GB UFS storage is expandable up to 1TB via microSD card. There's a dedicated microSD slot [citation:12].
Is the battery really 12 hours?
Digit's one-month test found the battery life "impressive" for daily use. Real-world backup depends on usage — expect 8-10 hours with mixed browsing and video, and closer to 12 hours with light document work [citation:3].
Can I play games on it?
Only light Android games. It's not a gaming laptop. For BGMI, Genshin, or PC games, look elsewhere [citation:3].
Is the trackpad really that bad?
Multiple reviewers and Digit's hands-on test confirm the trackpad is inconsistent and erratic. Budget for an external mouse — it dramatically improves the experience [citation:1][citation:3].
What's the return policy?
Some buyers report difficulty returning the device — Amazon may ask for a "Denial Letter" from the brand first. Check the current Amazon return policy before buying [citation:1].
