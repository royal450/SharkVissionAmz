MacBook Neo vs Snapdragon X vs Intel Core Ultra — which is best?
MacBook Neo — best for Apple ecosystem, ease of use, and long battery. Snapdragon X (HP OmniBook 5) — best for AI workloads, OLED display, and battery efficiency.
