How to choose a TV

Buying a TV is not about the biggest number on the box. It's about what fits your room, your viewing distance, and how you actually watch. Here's the honest breakdown.

Screen size

Screen size by room

For a small bedroom (viewing distance ~6–7 ft), 32–43 inch is comfortable. For a living room (~8–10 ft), 43–55 inch works well. For larger halls (10–12 ft+), 55–65 inch or more makes sense. Bigger isn't always better — a huge TV too close will feel overwhelming and may look softer on lower-quality content.

Panel type

LED vs QLED vs OLED

LED is the budget-friendly baseline — good brightness, fine for daily watching. QLED adds a quantum-dot layer for punchier colour and brightness, great for bright rooms. OLED gives perfect blacks and wide viewing angles, but costs more and can be less bright in very sunny rooms. Match the panel to your room, not to the marketing.

Resolution

4K is the new normal

4K (Ultra HD) is now standard at most price points and worth it for 43 inch and above. Full HD still makes sense on smaller 32 inch sets. 8K is not worth the premium for most Indian buyers today — content is limited and prices are high.

Refresh rate

60Hz vs 120Hz

60Hz is fine for movies, daily soaps, and casual streaming. 120Hz helps for fast sports and console gaming. Watch out for "effective refresh rate" marketing numbers — check the native panel refresh rate, not the interpolated one.

Smart TV OS

Android TV, Google TV, or something else

Google TV / Android TV gives the widest app support in India (YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar, SonyLIV, etc.). Some budget brands use their own OS with fewer apps — check app support before buying. A smooth OS with regular updates matters more than a long feature list.

Sound

Don't ignore the speakers

Most slim TVs have average built-in sound. If you care about movies or music, budget for a soundbar or plan to use external speakers. Check rated output (in watts) and whether there's Dolby support — but trust real user feedback more than spec sheets.

Budget

Budget ranges in India

Entry-level 32 inch sets start low; 43 inch 4K is the popular sweet spot; 55 inch and above moves into premium territory. OLED and large QLED sets sit at the top. Set a hard budget first, then pick the panel and size that fit inside it.

Checklist

Before you buy

Verify the native resolution and refresh rate, check the number of HDMI and USB ports, confirm the OS and app support, read verified user reviews for panel uniformity and after-sales service, and always re-check the live price on Amazon or Flipkart — prices in India move fast.

TV buying FAQ

Which screen size is best for an Indian living room?

For a typical 8–10 ft viewing distance, 43–55 inch is the sweet spot. For a bedroom with a shorter distance, 32–43 inch is more comfortable. Match the size to the viewing distance, not to the biggest number available.

Is QLED worth it over a normal LED TV?

If your room is bright or you watch a lot of colourful content, QLED usually looks noticeably better. In a dim room and on a tight budget, a good LED TV is perfectly fine. Don't pay extra for a label alone — check the panel and real reviews.
