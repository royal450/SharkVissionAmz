About ShopMaze

An independent tech review site built for Indian shoppers — no sponsored picks, no fake ratings, just careful research you can trust.

Why ShopMaze exists

Buying a gadget in India is confusing. Every site says "best laptop under ₹30,000" — but most of them just copy Amazon specs and paste affiliate links. None of them tell you what you're actually getting for your money, or what you're giving up.

ShopMaze started because I got tired of that. I wanted a site that tells you the truth — the specs, the real-world performance, the trade-offs, and whether the price makes sense in 2025.

Every guide here is built from hands-on research: 50+ verified user reviews, spec sheets, price history on Amazon and Flipkart, and my own experience helping friends and family pick the right gadget.

What we stand for

01
No sponsored picks

Brands can't pay to appear on ShopMaze. Every recommendation is based on research and real-world value — not on who offers the highest commission.

02
Real prices, not "starting from"

We show you what a product actually costs today on Amazon or Flipkart — not a fake "starting from" price that never exists in stock.

03
We list the cons

Every product has a trade-off. Cheap laptops have weak displays. Budget phones have slower chips. We tell you upfront, so you don't get surprised later.

04
Updated, always

Prices change. New models launch. We re-check every guide regularly so you don't buy something outdated or overpriced.

How we research each guide

Every buying guide on ShopMaze follows the same 5-step process:

1
Shortlist 20+ models

For each price range, we shortlist every laptop, phone, or TV that's actually available in India — not just the ones with the biggest marketing budget.

2
Compare specs and real-world reviews

We read 50+ verified reviews on Amazon, Flipkart, Reddit, and YouTube. We cross-check what reviewers say vs. what the spec sheet promises.

3
Track price history

We check 3-6 months of price data to make sure the current price is genuinely good — not a fake "MRP discount" that all retailers pretend to offer.

4
Pick the top 3 by use-case

Different people need different things. A student needs battery life. An office worker needs a good keyboard. We pick a "best overall," "best value," and "best for specific use" — not just one winner.

5
List the trade-offs honestly

Every pick includes a "Cons" section. If a laptop has an average display, we say so. If a phone's camera is bad in low light, we say so.

Editorial policy

How we make money (and how we don't)

ShopMaze earns through affiliate commissions — when you buy through our Amazon or Flipkart links, we get a small cut at no extra cost to you.

That's it. We don't sell sponsored placements. We don't accept payment for positive reviews. We don't let brands influence which products we recommend.

If a product is bad, we say it's bad — even if it has a high affiliate commission. Trust is worth more than a one-time sale.

If you ever spot an error, an outdated price, or a product we should include, please email us or message us on WhatsApp. We update guides based on reader feedback.
