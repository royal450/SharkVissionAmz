Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about ShopMaze MCP servers.

What is a ShopMaze MCP server?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers that let AI agents fully control and automate your business — websites, marketing, emails, scheduling, and more. We build and host them for you.

Who is this for?

Agencies, founders, and businesses that want full AI automation without hiring a large technical team. Ideal if you want AI to build, market, and run operations while you focus on strategy.

Do you provide the team and servers?

Yes. We have a dedicated team of 12+ members and multiple production servers. You get reliable infrastructure and support, not just code.

Can AI really build the website and do marketing?

Yes. Once connected, AI creates and maintains your website, runs marketing campaigns, sends emails, and executes daily scheduled tasks — all through the MCP tools we provide.

How do daily triggers work?

You (or AI) set schedules once. The system runs your workflows every day — reports, content, outreach, status updates — and emails the results to you automatically.

How do I get started?

Contact us on WhatsApp. Tell us about your business. We set up the right MCP servers and walk you through connecting your AI tools. Fast onboarding.
