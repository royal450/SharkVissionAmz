Quick Questions and Answers

Does it support fast charging?
It supports 15W Fast Charging. However, due to the massive 6300mAh battery, users report it takes a considerable amount of time to reach 100 percent. It also supports 6W reverse charging.
Is the display Full HD or HD+?
It features a 6.74-inch HD+ LCD display with a 90Hz refresh rate and 180Hz touch sampling rate. It is not Full HD+.
Can I play heavy games like BGMI or COD on this?
It handles light gaming smoothly. However, heavy games like BGMI or Call of Duty may experience lag or frame drops due to the entry-level Unisoc T7250 processor.
Is it durable and water-resistant?
Yes, it comes with an IP54 rating for dust and water resistance, along with military-grade shock resistance (tested for 1.8m drops), making it highly durable for daily rough use.
